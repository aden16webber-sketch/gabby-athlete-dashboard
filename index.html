Half a banana or a few dates, sipped water. It's humid (~65-68%) even at a cool start temp — hydrate deliberately, not just when thirsty.

After

Soy milk, a pea-protein shake, or a tofu/tempeh meal with rice. The day warms into the 80s with real humidity, so keep drinking water well past the race itself.
